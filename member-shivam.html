The Story

The Voice
of Black Heart

Shivam Rishant is the emotional center of Black Heart. As lead vocalist, he is the first thing a crowd hears — and the last thing they forget. His voice moves between rock grit and Sufi softness with a ease that takes years to develop and a lifetime to perfect.

Born with a natural ear for melody, Shivam grew up immersed in Bollywood music before discovering rock. That collision — the lush, emotion-driven world of Indian film music and the raw power of rock — is exactly what makes his voice so distinctive.

On stage, he is a presence. Off stage, he is a student — always listening, always refining, always pushing himself toward a performance that hits harder than the last.

Shivam's range is one of the widest in the Delhi music scene. He can deliver a delicate Sufi phrase with total control, then pivot to a soaring rock chorus without missing a beat. That versatility is what allows Black Heart to move so freely between genres.

He approaches each song as its own world — learning the story behind it, understanding the emotion in it, and then making it his own. The result is a vocalist who doesn't just sing songs, but inhabits them.
