The Story

The Man
Behind the Band

Tarang Verma is the architect of Black Heart — the vision, the drive, and the discipline that holds six distinct musical personalities together. Born and raised in Indore, his relationship with music began in his teens when he first picked up a guitar and realized that notes could carry weight that words couldn't.

As founder and director, Tarang doesn't just lead the band — he curates its identity. Every performance, every setlist, every aesthetic decision carries his fingerprint. His guitar work forms the spine of Black Heart's sound: heavy enough to anchor, melodic enough to soar.

He is the reason Black Heart exists. And his mission has always been the same — to make music that leaves a scar.

Tarang's approach to guitar is self-taught and instinct-driven. He draws from classic rock, Bollywood, and Sufi music — a collision that defines the Black Heart sound and sets it apart from every other band on the Indore circuit.

Off stage, he is a student of the craft — listening obsessively, experimenting constantly, and pushing every member of the band to find their best version of themselves. That relentless pursuit is what took Black Heart from late-night rehearsals to packed stages in under a year.
