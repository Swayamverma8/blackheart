The Story

The Sound
Between Worlds

Ritjeet Deepak brings something to Black Heart that no other rock band in Delhi has — the haunting, soul-penetrating sound of the bansuri. In a genre dominated by distortion and drums, Ritjeet's flute carves space for melody, breath, and stillness.

Trained in classical Indian music from a young age, Ritjeet spent years mastering the bansuri before discovering that its true power emerged in unexpected contexts. When Tarang brought him into Black Heart, the fit was immediate — the flute became the band's most distinctive voice.

His playing draws deeply from the Sufi tradition — improvisational, searching, and emotionally direct. But he can also pivot to precise melodic lines that lock in with the guitar and bass to create something cinematic and powerful.

Off stage, Ritjeet is the band's quiet philosopher. He rarely speaks first, but when he does, the band listens. His musical instincts are among the most developed in the group, and his willingness to experiment keeps Black Heart's sound from ever going stale.
